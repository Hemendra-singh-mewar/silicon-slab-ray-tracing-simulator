Gap equation

Snell's law

r = sin⁻¹[(nair/nsilicon) sin i]

Perpendicular gap

Gap = 2t tan(r) cos(i)

For the example shown:

t = 29.7 mm, i = 45°, nSi = 3.48 → Gap = 8.72 mm

Critical angle

For silicon to air:

θc = sin⁻¹(nair/nsilicon)

The animation increases the angle inside silicon towards θc. At the critical angle the refracted ray travels along the interface. Above it, total internal reflection occurs.
